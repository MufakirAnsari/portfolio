03 // EVALUATION RUBRIC & REVIEW ETHICS

My Review Methodology.

I view peer review not as a gatekeeping hurdle, but as a constructive engineering audit designed to elevate the clarity, empirical reproducibility, and lasting scientific contribution of each submission.

code

Algorithmic Reproducibility

Verifying that model architectures, training hyperparameters, random seeds, and loss formulations are documented with enough fidelity for an independent researcher to replicate the exact results.

speed

Compute & Efficiency Audit

Evaluating reported computational complexity, GPU hours, parameters, FLOPs, and inference latency. Checking whether claimed accuracy gains justify the computational footprint.

analytics

Statistical Significance

Requiring proper cross-validation, standard deviation reporting across multiple runs, and non-parametric hypothesis testing (Wilcoxon, t-tests) rather than single cherry-picked metrics.

balance

Constructive Feedback

Providing actionable, itemized suggestions for authors—highlighting specific experiments, missing baselines, or structural revisions that turn a promising paper into high-impact literature.
